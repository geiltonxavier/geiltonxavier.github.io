<div class="home-bio">
  <p><span class="home-name-wrap"><span class="home-name" tabindex="0">Geilton Xavier</span><span class="bio-portrait"><img src="/img/geilton-avatar.jpeg" alt="Geilton Xavier" loading="lazy"></span></span> is a software architect and engineer who likes software with <a href="https://doi.org/10.1145/361598.361623" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">clear boundaries</a>, sensible defaults, and as little <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220477127_No_Silver_Bullet_Essence_and_Accidents_of_Software_Engineering" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">accidental complexity</a> as possible.</p>
  <p>He believes systems should feel effortless for the people who use and build on them: clear structure, low cognitive load, and a straight path from intention to action.</p>
  <p>Today, he works at Siemens, designing and building software where architecture and product thinking meet.</p>
  <p>Previously, he worked at <a href="https://www.fidelidade.pt/" data-tooltip="Portugal's largest insurance company, covering auto, health, home, and life insurance." target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fidelidade</a>, <a href="https://www.softfinanca.com/en-us/" data-tooltip="A fintech company focused on core banking and financial platforms." target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SoftFinança</a>, <a href="https://www.edenred.com.br/" data-tooltip="A global benefits company behind meal and mobility vouchers." target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Edenred</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/vizir" data-tooltip="A software development studio building custom digital products for clients." target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vizir Software Studio</a>.</p>
