At work

Thinking about

Platform engineering, event-driven architecture, cloud infrastructure, AI-assisted software development, and the craft of making complex systems feel simple.

I'm also interested in how developers learn, and in the distance between a useful tool and one that quietly adds more friction.

Outside work

I live in Portugal, build small products, write about engineering, and experiment with ideas that may or may not become anything larger.
