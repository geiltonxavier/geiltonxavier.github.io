Geilton Xavier is a software architect and engineer who cares about simplicity, convention over configuration, and good design.

He believes systems should feel effortless for the people who use and build on them: clear structure, low cognitive load, and a straight path from intention to action.

Today, he works at Siemens, designing and building software where architecture and product thinking meet.

Previously, he worked at Fidelidade, SoftFinança, Edenred, and Vizir Software Studio.
